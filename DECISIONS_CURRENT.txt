9) Decision: Adopt sync/cont/stop as a standing ChatGPT<->Claude
   coordination protocol (expanded same-day from "sync" alone to all
   three keywords). sync = delta-oriented handoff checkpoint, never
   pauses work. cont = resume/continue immediately on the next safe
   unblocked increment, no waiting for scheduled checkpoints. stop =
   halt at next safe point, preserve state, publish where/why. None of
   the three override the commit gate, drift kill switch, privacy
   rule, an active blocker, or failed validation.
   Date: 2026-08-07
   Rationale: User-established convention, explicit and unambiguous.
   Keeps handoff refresh cheap (delta, not full rewrite) to minimize
   token use, per explicit instruction.
   Affected component: session coordination protocol (see
   CLAUDE_OVERNIGHT_AUTONOMOUS_PROMPT.txt, README.md "sync / cont /
   stop keywords")
   Status: Frozen (implemented) — operating protocol, not a Niklar
   trading/canonical rule.
